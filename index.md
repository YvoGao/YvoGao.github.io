## About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in **Software Engineering** at **Dalian University of Technology (DUT)**, where I also received my Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Software Engineering.

My research focuses on **few-shot learning**, **class-incremental learning**, and **vision-language model (VLM) adaptation**. I am particularly interested in developing data-efficient learning methods that enable pretrained multimodal models, such as CLIP and CLAP, to effectively adapt to new tasks and evolving categories with limited supervision.

## Research Interests

- **Few-Shot Learning:** few-shot image, audio, and text classification
- **Class-Incremental Learning:** few-shot class-incremental learning and continual adaptation
- **Multimodal Learning:** vision-language and audio-language models
- **Foundation Model Adaptation:** efficient adaptation of pretrained multimodal models with limited labeled data
